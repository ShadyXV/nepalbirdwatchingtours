Hello, I'm Krishna

I'm a nature guide from Kathmandu, and for the past 6 years I have been walking Nepal's forests, hills and grasslands with birders, wildlife lovers and photographers. I paint birds too, so I look at every one of them closely. Come out with me and I'll show you what I see.

More about Krishna

Our birding tours & services

  • Led birding tours

    Multi-day trips that I lead from the first morning to the last, planned around the birds and the season. We walk at a birder's pace, start at first light, and go where the birds are that month: the Annapurna forests, the grasslands of Chitwan, the hills above Kathmandu.

    See the trips

  • Day tours

    Only have one day? I'll pick you up before dawn and we'll spend it in the forests around the Kathmandu Valley, or in Chitwan or Bardia if you're already there. Easy walking, a packed lunch, and more birds than you would ever guess.

    See the day tours

  • Birding specialist on treks

    Already planning a trek? I can join your group as the birding and nature specialist while your trek guide looks after the logistics. Same mountains, same trail, and someone beside you who knows every call in the forest.
