Langtang Birdwatching Trek

From a deep forest gorge to the yak pastures below Langtang Lirung, a short drive from Kathmandu.

10 days, up to 4,773 m, and 69 birds recorded on this route.

Best months: March to May and October to November.

Start the journeyDay by day, on the map

1 / 5

The Himalayan park closest to Kathmandu. A deep forest gorge full of niltavas, minivets and the chance of a Red-headed Trogon, then yak pastures under Langtang Lirung, where Ibisbill feed on the river shingle and Snow Pigeons wheel overhead. Ten unhurried days, with a spare day at Kyanjin Gompa for the high birds.

A day on this trip

Birding starts at dawn around the lodge, then four to six hours of walking through the gorge forest, unhurried, with long stops for flocks. Above Langtang village the days are short, and the afternoons are free for photographs, sketching or the river flats.

The way, day by day

  1. Day 1
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Large-billed Crow

    1. Airport pickup, hotel check-in and the trip briefing.

    Birds 10: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  2. Day 2
    Syabrubesi1,460 m · Wallcreeper, Plumbeous Water Redstart

    1. A scenic drive (7–8 hours) to the trailhead village of Syabrubesi.

    Birds 14: Plumbeous Water Redstart, Grey Treepie, Himalayan Bulbul, Blue Whistling-Thrush, Oriental White-eye, Great Barbet, Brown Dipper, Spotted Forktail, Slaty-headed Parakeet, Verditer Flycatcher, Black Bulbul, Crested Kingfisher, White-capped Redstart, Wallcreeper

    Mammals 1: Himalayan Goral

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  3. Day 3
    Lama Hotel2,470 m · Red-headed Trogon, Rufous-bellied Niltava

    1. Through the warm lower gorge of the Langtang Khola, where forktails work the stream and mixed flocks move through the trees.

    2. A trek through dense subtropical and temperate forest, one of the best birding stretches of the trek, watching for minivets, niltavas and woodpeckers.

    Birds 22: Black Bulbul, Grey-hooded Warbler, Whiskered Yuhina, Rufous Sibia, Scarlet Minivet, Long-tailed Minivet, Rufous-bellied Niltava, Large Niltava, Greater Yellownape, Spotted Forktail, Little Forktail, Red-headed Trogon, Green-backed Tit, Blue-winged Minla, Chestnut-crowned Laughingthrush, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Rufous-bellied Woodpecker, Kalij Pheasant, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Yellow-billed Blue Magpie, Koklass Pheasant, Satyr Tragopan

    Mammals 6: Assamese Macaque, Himalayan Langur, Yellow-throated Marten, Himalayan Goral, Red Panda, Himalayan Black Bear

    Plants 1: Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  4. Day 4
    Langtang Village3,430 m · Himalayan Monal, Blood Pheasant

    1. The forest opens out at Ghoda Tabela, and the first views of Langtang Lirung appear up the valley.

    2. We continue upward through forest transitioning to alpine shrubland, with views of Langtang Lirung opening up, to Langtang village.

    Birds 20: Rufous-vented Tit, Grey-crested Tit, White-browed Fulvetta, Spotted Nutcracker, Himalayan Monal, White-collared Blackbird, Himalayan Griffon, Lammergeier, Blood Pheasant, Rufous-breasted Accentor, Large-billed Crow, Yellow-billed Chough, Red-billed Chough, Himalayan White-browed Rosefinch, Dark-breasted Rosefinch, Grey-backed Shrike, White-capped Redstart, Common Rosefinch, Hodgson's Redstart, Snow Pigeon

    Mammals 3: Himalayan Goral, Himalayan Tahr, Red Panda

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  5. Day 5
    Kyanjin Gompa3,870 m · Ibisbill, Grandala

    1. A shorter trekking day reaching Kyanjin Gompa, with time to explore high-altitude habitat for snow pigeons and alpine species.

    Birds 16: Yellow-billed Chough, Red-billed Chough, Rufous-breasted Accentor, Plain Mountain Finch, Snow Pigeon, Robin Accentor, White-winged Redstart, Himalayan White-browed Rosefinch, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Brown Dipper, Himalayan Snowcock, Golden Eagle, Ibisbill, Alpine Accentor, Grandala

    Mammals 2: Himalayan Tahr, Large-eared Pika

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  6. Day 6
    Kyanjin Ri4,773 m · Wallcreeper, Lammergeier

    1. An optional hike up Kyanjin Ri (4,773 m) for panoramic Himalayan views and high-altitude birding, or a rest day in the valley.

    Birds 10: Snow Pigeon, Yellow-billed Chough, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Himalayan Snowcock, Golden Eagle, Wallcreeper, White-winged Redstart, Alpine Accentor, Grandala

    Mammals 2: Large-eared Pika, Blue Sheep

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m), Langtang Himal (0 m)

  7. Day 7
    Lama Hotel2,470 m · Rufous-bellied Niltava, Satyr Tragopan

    1. We descend back through the forest zones, birding en route in daylight for full coverage.

    Birds 16: Rufous Sibia, Whiskered Yuhina, Green-backed Tit, Blue-winged Minla, Chestnut-crowned Laughingthrush, Rufous-bellied Niltava, Large Niltava, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Rufous-bellied Woodpecker, Long-tailed Minivet, Scarlet Minivet, Kalij Pheasant, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Yellow-billed Blue Magpie, Koklass Pheasant, Satyr Tragopan

    Mammals 5: Himalayan Langur, Himalayan Goral, Yellow-throated Marten, Red Panda, Himalayan Black Bear

    Plants 1: Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  8. Day 8
    Syabrubesi1,460 m · Wallcreeper, Plumbeous Water Redstart

    1. The final descent to the trailhead village.

    Birds 14: Plumbeous Water Redstart, Grey Treepie, Himalayan Bulbul, Blue Whistling-Thrush, Oriental White-eye, Great Barbet, Brown Dipper, Spotted Forktail, Slaty-headed Parakeet, Verditer Flycatcher, Black Bulbul, Crested Kingfisher, White-capped Redstart, Wallcreeper

    Mammals 1: Himalayan Goral

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  9. Day 9
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Large-billed Crow

    1. The return drive to Kathmandu, and a free evening.

    Birds 10: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  10. Day 10
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Large-billed Crow

    1. Final breakfast and the departure transfer.
