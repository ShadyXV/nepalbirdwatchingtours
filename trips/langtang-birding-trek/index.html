Langtang Birdwatching Trek

From a deep forest gorge to the yak pastures below Langtang Lirung, a short drive from Kathmandu.

Langtang National Park, the closest Himalayan national park to Kathmandu, offers superb birding through dense forest, glacial valleys, and high alpine pasture, all within a manageable 10-day itinerary.

Highlights

  • Only a short drive from Kathmandu to the trailhead
  • Forest zones rich in pheasants, laughingthrushes, and Himalayan specialties
  • Dramatic glacial valley scenery beneath Langtang Lirung
  • Combines trekking and dedicated birding activities

The way, day by day

  1. Day 1Kathmandu

    Day 1→

    1,400 m · 1 night(s) · hotel

    1. Stay: hotel. Hotel in Kathmandu

      Airport pickup, hotel check-in and the trip briefing.

    10 birds possible

    Birds: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  2. Day 2Syabrubesi

    Day 2→

    1,460 m · 1 night(s) · lodge

    1. Stay: lodge. Lodge in the trailhead village by the river

      A scenic drive (7–8 hours) to the trailhead village of Syabrubesi.

    14 birds possible

    Birds: Plumbeous Water Redstart, Grey Treepie, Himalayan Bulbul, Blue Whistling-Thrush, Oriental White-eye, Great Barbet, Brown Dipper, Spotted Forktail, Slaty-headed Parakeet, Verditer Flycatcher, Black Bulbul, Crested Kingfisher, White-capped Redstart, Wallcreeper

    Mammals: Himalayan Goral

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  3. Day 3Lama Hotel

    Day 3→

    2,470 m · 1 night(s) · teahouse

    1. Through the warm lower gorge of the Langtang Khola, where forktails work the stream and mixed flocks move through the trees.

    2. Stay: teahouse. Teahouse in a forest clearing by the river

      A trek through dense subtropical and temperate forest, one of the best birding stretches of the trek, watching for minivets, niltavas and woodpeckers.

    22 birds possible

    Birds: Black Bulbul, Grey-hooded Warbler, Whiskered Yuhina, Rufous Sibia, Scarlet Minivet, Long-tailed Minivet, Rufous-bellied Niltava, Large Niltava, Greater Yellownape, Spotted Forktail, Little Forktail, Red-headed Trogon, Green-backed Tit, Blue-winged Minla, Chestnut-crowned Laughingthrush, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Rufous-bellied Woodpecker, Kalij Pheasant, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Yellow-billed Blue Magpie, Koklass Pheasant, Satyr Tragopan

    Mammals: Assamese Macaque, Himalayan Langur, Yellow-throated Marten, Himalayan Goral, Red Panda, Himalayan Black Bear

    Plants: Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  4. Day 4Langtang Village

    Day 4→

    3,430 m · 1 night(s) · teahouse

    1. The forest opens out at Ghoda Tabela, and the first views of Langtang Lirung appear up the valley.

    2. Stay: teahouse. Family teahouse in the rebuilt village

      We continue upward through forest transitioning to alpine shrubland, with views of Langtang Lirung opening up, to Langtang village.

    20 birds possible

    Birds: Rufous-vented Tit, Grey-crested Tit, White-browed Fulvetta, Spotted Nutcracker, Himalayan Monal, White-collared Blackbird, Himalayan Griffon, Lammergeier, Blood Pheasant, Rufous-breasted Accentor, Large-billed Crow, Yellow-billed Chough, Red-billed Chough, Himalayan White-browed Rosefinch, Dark-breasted Rosefinch, Grey-backed Shrike, White-capped Redstart, Common Rosefinch, Hodgson's Redstart, Snow Pigeon

    Mammals: Himalayan Goral, Himalayan Tahr, Red Panda

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  5. Day 5Kyanjin Gompa

    Day 5→

    3,870 m · 2 night(s) · teahouse

    1. Stay: teahouse. Teahouse by the monastery and cheese factory; two nights

      A shorter trekking day reaching Kyanjin Gompa, with time to explore high-altitude habitat for snow pigeons and alpine species.

    16 birds possible

    Birds: Yellow-billed Chough, Red-billed Chough, Rufous-breasted Accentor, Plain Mountain Finch, Snow Pigeon, Robin Accentor, White-winged Redstart, Himalayan White-browed Rosefinch, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Brown Dipper, Himalayan Snowcock, Golden Eagle, Ibisbill, Alpine Accentor, Grandala

    Mammals: Himalayan Tahr, Large-eared Pika

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  6. Day 6Kyanjin Ri

    Day 6→

    4,773 m

    1. An optional hike up Kyanjin Ri (4,773 m) for panoramic Himalayan views and high-altitude birding, or a rest day in the valley.

    10 birds possible

    Birds: Snow Pigeon, Yellow-billed Chough, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Himalayan Snowcock, Golden Eagle, Wallcreeper, White-winged Redstart, Alpine Accentor, Grandala

    Mammals: Large-eared Pika, Blue Sheep

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m), Langtang Himal (0 m)

    Day 7→

    2,470 m · 1 night(s) · teahouse

    1. Stay: teahouse. Back at the Lama Hotel teahouse

      We descend back through the forest zones, birding en route in daylight for full coverage.

    16 birds possible

    Birds: Rufous Sibia, Whiskered Yuhina, Green-backed Tit, Blue-winged Minla, Chestnut-crowned Laughingthrush, Rufous-bellied Niltava, Large Niltava, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Rufous-bellied Woodpecker, Long-tailed Minivet, Scarlet Minivet, Kalij Pheasant, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Yellow-billed Blue Magpie, Koklass Pheasant, Satyr Tragopan

    Mammals: Himalayan Langur, Himalayan Goral, Yellow-throated Marten, Red Panda, Himalayan Black Bear

    Plants: Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

    Day 8→

    1. Stay: lodge. Lodge in the trailhead village

      The final descent to the trailhead village.

    14 birds possible

    Mammals: Himalayan Goral
