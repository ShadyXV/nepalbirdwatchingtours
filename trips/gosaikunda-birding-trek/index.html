Gosaikunda Birdwatching Trek

Through pheasant forest to the sacred lakes at 4,380 m, and down a quiet trail back to Kathmandu.

10 days, up to 4,610 m, and 74 birds recorded on this route.

Best months: March to May and October to November.

Start the journeyDay by day, on the map

1 / 5

A pilgrims' trail through pheasant forest to the sacred lakes at 4,380 m, over the Laurebina Pass and down the quiet Helambu side to Kathmandu. Monals and Blood Pheasants in the rhododendron, Snow Pigeons, Robin Accentors and winter Grandalas around the lakes, and Nepal's only endemic bird, the Spiny Babbler, on the last hill. For fit trekkers who want the high country without the crowds.

Birds to look for

None of them is promised: wild birds keep their own plans.

    Pyrrhula erythrocephala

    “A soft-voiced bullfinch with an orange-red head, feeding quietly in the rhododendron.”

  • Lammergeier

    Gypaetus barbatus

    Gosaikunda · 4,380 m · usually seen

    “A long-winged, rust-chested giant that sails along the cliffs and drops bones to crack them.”

  • Snow Pigeon

    Columba leuconota

    Gosaikunda · 4,380 m · usually seen

    “White and slate flocks that swirl across the cliffs of the sanctuary.”

  • Robin Accentor

    Prunella rubeculoides

    Gosaikunda · 4,380 m · usually seen

    “A neat grey-headed accentor with a robin-red breast, at home around the high lakes and yak huts.”

  • Grandala

    Grandala coelicolor

    Gosaikunda · 4,380 m · November to March · sometimes seen

    “Flocks of blue over the frozen shore”

  • Himalayan Snowcock

    Tetraogallus himalayensis

    Gosaikunda · 4,380 m · sometimes seen

    “A big mountain game bird whose ringing whistles bounce around the high amphitheatre.”

  • Satyr Tragopan

    Tragopan satyra

    Kutumsang · 2,470 m · sometimes seen

  • Spiny Babbler

    Chisapani · 2,215 m · sometimes seen

A day on this trip

Mornings start at sunrise in the forest, when the pheasants call. We walk five or six hours a day, steep in places and slow above 4,000 m, stopping wherever there's a flock; the high days are short so there's time to rest and acclimatise.

The way, day by day

  1. Day 1
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Large-billed Crow

    1. Airport pickup, hotel check-in and the trip briefing.

    Birds 10: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  2. Day 2
    Dhunche1,960 m · Grey Treepie, Great Barbet

    1. The drive to the trailhead area in the Langtang region, up the Trishuli valley to Dhunche.

    Birds 13: Himalayan Bulbul, Grey Treepie, Great Barbet, Oriental White-eye, Black-throated Tit, Blue Whistling-Thrush, Plumbeous Water Redstart, Verditer Flycatcher, Long-tailed Minivet, Slaty-headed Parakeet, Rufous Sibia, Himalayan Griffon, Kalij Pheasant

    Mammals 2: Himalayan Goral, Himalayan Langur

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  3. Day 3
    Chandanbari (Sing Gompa)3,330 m · Himalayan Monal, Blood Pheasant

    1. We begin trekking through dense rhododendron and oak forest, prime habitat for laughingthrushes and pheasants, reaching Sing Gompa. The forest around the monastery is also known for red pandas.

    Birds 16: Grey-crested Tit, Rufous-vented Tit, White-browed Fulvetta, Spotted Nutcracker, Stripe-throated Yuhina, Himalayan Monal, Spotted Laughingthrush, Hoary-throated Barwing, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Rufous-bellied Woodpecker, Collared Grosbeak, White-collared Blackbird, Chestnut-crowned Laughingthrush, Blood Pheasant, Koklass Pheasant, Red-headed Bullfinch

    Mammals 4: Yellow-throated Marten, Red Panda, Himalayan Musk Deer, Himalayan Black Bear

    Plants 2: Tree Rhododendron, Bearded Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  4. Day 4
    Lauribina3,910 m · Lammergeier, Grandala

    1. We continue climbing through forest thinning into alpine shrubland, watching the habitat and bird community shift with altitude.

    Birds 13: Rufous-breasted Accentor, White-browed Fulvetta, Large-billed Crow, Yellow-billed Chough, Himalayan Monal, Blood Pheasant, Dark-breasted Rosefinch, Himalayan White-browed Rosefinch, White-collared Blackbird, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Golden Eagle, Grandala

    Mammals 1: Himalayan Tahr

    Plants 2: Bell-flowered Rhododendron, Lepidote Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  5. Day 5
    Gosaikunda4,380 m · Snow Pigeon, Robin Accentor

    1. We reach the sacred lakes of Gosaikunda, with time to explore the high-altitude terrain for alpine specialists.

    Birds 12: Yellow-billed Chough, Red-billed Chough, Alpine Accentor, Robin Accentor, Plain Mountain Finch, Snow Pigeon, Lammergeier, Himalayan Snowcock, White-winged Redstart, Brown Dipper, Grandala, Ruddy Shelduck

    Mammals 2: Himalayan Tahr, Large-eared Pika

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  6. Day 6
    Ridge above Gosaikunda4,600 m · Wallcreeper, Lammergeier

    1. A day around the lake area, with the option of a higher viewpoint hike and birding for high-altitude species. It also helps with acclimatization before the pass.

    Birds 9: Snow Pigeon, Alpine Accentor, Yellow-billed Chough, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Himalayan Snowcock, Golden Eagle, Wallcreeper, Grandala

    Mammals 1: Large-eared Pika

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  7. Day 7
    Laurebina Pass · Ghopte3,430 m · Himalayan Monal, Blood Pheasant

    1. We cross the Laurebina Pass (4,610 m), the highest point of the trek, among cairns and small lakes.

    2. Then the descent toward Ghopte, a quieter, less-trekked stretch.

    Birds 19: Alpine Accentor, Robin Accentor, Snow Pigeon, Yellow-billed Chough, Red-billed Chough, Plain Mountain Finch, Lammergeier, Himalayan Snowcock, White-winged Redstart, Rufous-breasted Accentor, White-browed Fulvetta, Spotted Nutcracker, Grey-crested Tit, Rufous-vented Tit, Himalayan Monal, Blood Pheasant, White-collared Blackbird, Dark-breasted Rosefinch, Red-headed Bullfinch

    Mammals 4: Large-eared Pika, Himalayan Tahr, Red Panda, Himalayan Musk Deer

    Plants 1: Bell-flowered Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  8. Day 8
    Kutumsang2,470 m · Satyr Tragopan, Whiskered Yuhina

    1. A short climb to the ridge at Tharepati, where the Helambu trail turns south.

    2. We continue descending through forest toward lower elevations, with strong birding in the mixed forest zone, to Kutumsang.

    Birds 23: Himalayan Monal, Blood Pheasant, Spotted Nutcracker, Yellow-billed Chough, Large-billed Crow, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Rufous-breasted Accentor, Rufous Sibia, Whiskered Yuhina, Green-backed Tit, Blue-winged Minla, Great Barbet, Chestnut-crowned Laughingthrush, Striated Laughingthrush, Kalij Pheasant, Yellow-billed Blue Magpie, Mrs. Gould's Sunbird, Scarlet Minivet, Satyr Tragopan, Hill Partridge, Great Parrotbill, Himalayan Cuckoo

    Mammals 2: Himalayan Langur, Barking Deer

    Plants 2: Bell-flowered Rhododendron, Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Langtang Lirung (7,227 m)

  9. Day 9
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Spiny Babbler, Grey-hooded Warbler

    1. Down the ridge and into the Shivapuri forest at Chisapani, the last hill before Kathmandu.

    2. The final descent to the roadhead at Sundarijal, and the drive back to the city.

    3. Back in Kathmandu for the night.

    Birds 28: Himalayan Bulbul, Rufous Sibia, Whiskered Yuhina, Black-throated Tit, Grey-hooded Warbler, Great Barbet, Kalij Pheasant, White-throated Laughingthrush, Red-billed Blue Magpie, Black Eagle, Himalayan Griffon, Spiny Babbler, Plumbeous Water Redstart, Blue Whistling-Thrush, Red-vented Bulbul, Grey Wagtail, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Spotted Forktail, Little Forktail, Verditer Flycatcher, White-capped Redstart, Brown Dipper, Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Spotted Dove, Eurasian Kestrel, Egyptian Vulture

    Mammals 1: Assamese Macaque

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  10. Day 10
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Large-billed Crow

    1. Final breakfast and the departure transfer.

    Birds 10: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)
