Kathmandu Birding Day: Phulchowki & Godawari

Kathmandu's best birding mountain in a single day: forested slopes at dawn, Godawari garden at midday.

Short on time but still want to experience world-class birdwatching in Kathmandu? Our one-day Kathmandu birding tour takes you straight from the city into the forested hills of Phulchowki and Godawari, one of the richest single-day birding sites in Nepal, with over 300 species recorded in the area.

Highlights

  • Spot 80–120 bird species in a single day, including forest specialists and valley wetland birds
  • Explore Phulchowki Hill, the highest point on the Kathmandu Valley rim
  • Guided by an expert local birder skilled at identifying calls
  • Perfect add-on for travelers with a short Kathmandu layover before or after a trek

The way, day by day

  1. Day 1National Botanical Garden, Godawari

    Day 1→

    1,500 m

    1. We depart Kathmandu before sunrise (around 5:30–6:00 AM) to reach the birding sites while activity is highest, with a packed breakfast and hot tea or coffee provided en route. Then we drive up the forested slopes of Phulchowki, stopping at productive spots along the road. Look for laughingthrushes, minivets, yuhinas, niltavas and, with luck, the elusive Spiny Babbler, Nepal's only endemic bird.

    2. Higher up the road, the forest turns cool and tall, with rhododendrons, orchids on the oak limbs and the chance of a Spotted Laughingthrush or Great Parrotbill in the bamboo. Flowers draw sunbirds in the spring.

    3. By mid-morning we reach the summit at 2,782 m, with a wide view across the Kathmandu Valley and, on a clear day, the white wall of the Langtang and Jugal Himal. Raptors start to circle in the thermals as the day warms.

    4. On the way down we stop for a packed lunch at the little temple, where a rushing stream cuts through the forest. Forktails and redstarts bob on the wet rocks, while niltavas and thrushes feed in the shady clearing.

    5. After lunch we continue to Godawari Botanical Garden, where mixed forest and open lawns attract flycatchers, barbets, woodpeckers and sunbirds. We wrap up the checklist over tea before dropping you back at your hotel by early evening (around 5:00–6:00 PM).
