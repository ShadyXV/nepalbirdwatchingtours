Bardiya Wildlife Safari and Birdwatching

Four full days in Bardiya's tiger country, timed around the best light at dawn and dusk.

Bardiya National Park, the largest national park in Nepal's lowlands and the least crowded, has one of the highest tiger densities in South Asia. This 9-day photography-focused tour is built around maximizing your chances of photographing wild Bengal tigers, alongside rhinos, elephants, and outstanding birdlife.

Highlights

  • Extended multi-day jeep safaris in Bardiya's core tiger habitat
  • Guided by naturalists experienced in wildlife photography logistics (positioning, light, patience protocols)
  • River activities for gharial crocodiles and riverine birds
  • Far fewer tourists than Chitwan, meaning quieter, more natural sightings

The way, day by day

  1. Day 1Kathmandu

    Day 1→

    1,400 m · 1 night(s) · hotel

    1. Stay: hotel. Hotel in Kathmandu

      Airport pickup, hotel check-in, and a photography-focused briefing (gear checklist, safari etiquette, target species).

    10 birds possible

    Birds: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  2. Day 2Thakurdwara

    Day 2→

    170 m · 6 night(s) · lodge

    1. A morning flight to Nepalgunj, then the drive to Bardiya National Park.

    2. Stay: lodge. Jungle lodge at the park edge; six nights in the same room

      Our base for six nights at the park's edge. An afternoon walk near the lodge to scout light conditions and river access points.

    21 birds possible

    Birds: Black Kite, Cattle Egret, Asian Pied Starling, Common Myna, Indian Pond-Heron, Black Drongo, Red-wattled Lapwing, Indian Roller, Spotted Owlet, Green Bee-eater, Long-tailed Shrike, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Coppersmith Barbet, Rufous Treepie, Rose-ringed Parakeet, Lineated Barbet, Greater Coucal, Indian Grey Hornbill, Common Hoopoe, Asian Koel

    Mammals: Rhesus Macaque

    Plants: Silk-cotton Tree

  3. Day 3Bardiya Grasslands

    Day 3→

    180 m

    1. Four full days of jeep and walking safaris across different zones of the park, timed around dawn and dusk light for photography. We start in the open grasslands, where deer herds graze at first light and tigers hunt the edges.

    10 birds possible

    Mammals: Spotted Deer, Wild Boar, Hog Deer, Swamp Deer, Nilgai, Northern Plains Gray Langur, Greater One-horned Rhinoceros, Asian Elephant, Bengal Tiger, Leopard

    Birds: Indian Peafowl, Red Junglefowl, Plain Prinia, Black Francolin, Striated Grassbird, Chestnut-capped Babbler, Crested Serpent-Eagle, Bengal Florican, Sarus Crane, Pied Harrier

    Plants: Kans Grass

  4. Day 4Karnali River (Geruwa)

    Day 4→

    155 m

    1. A river float on the Geruwa, the eastern channel of the Karnali, for gharial and riverine birds. From the banks we watch the open sand for tigers and elephants crossing in the evening light.

    17 birds possible

    Birds: River Lapwing, River Tern, Great Thick-knee, Pied Kingfisher, White-throated Kingfisher, Little Cormorant, Great Egret, Woolly-necked Stork, Oriental Darter, Crested Kingfisher, Asian Openbill, Grey-headed Fish-Eagle, Black-necked Stork, Brown Fish-Owl, Ruddy Shelduck, Common Sandpiper, Ibisbill

    Reptiles: Gharial, Mugger Crocodile

    Mammals: Ganges River Dolphin, Smooth-coated Otter, Asian Elephant, Bengal Tiger

  5. Day 5Sal Forest Interior

    Day 5→

    200 m

    1. A full day by jeep deep into the sal forest, with long quiet waits at waterholes and clearings. Woodpeckers, hornbills and raptors keep the camera busy between tiger searches.

    16 birds possible

    Birds: Greater Racket-tailed Drongo, Jungle Babbler, Common Iora, Black-hooded Oriole, Great Slaty Woodpecker, Greater Flameback, Lesser Yellownape, Oriental Pied Hornbill, Small Minivet, Changeable Hawk-Eagle, Great Hornbill, Red-headed Vulture, White-rumped Vulture, White-rumped Shama, Indian Pitta, Asian Paradise-Flycatcher

    Mammals: Spotted Deer, Sambar, Barking Deer, Northern Plains Gray Langur, Asian Elephant, Bengal Tiger, Sloth Bear, Leopard

    Plants: Sal

  6. Day 6Riverine Forest, Karnali Floodplain

    Day 6→

    160 m

    1. A walking safari through the riverine forest and floodplain, quiet and close to the ground, with rhinos in the wet grass and kingfishers along the channels.

    10 birds possible

    Birds: Red Junglefowl, Jungle Babbler, White-throated Kingfisher, Lesser Flameback, Fulvous-breasted Woodpecker, Tickell's Blue Flycatcher, Lineated Barbet, Stork-billed Kingfisher, Brown Fish-Owl, Orange-headed Thrush

    Mammals: Rhesus Macaque, Spotted Deer, Greater One-horned Rhinoceros, Asian Elephant, Bengal Tiger

    Reptiles: Bengal Monitor, Indian Rock Python

    Butterflies: Common Mormon

  7. Day 7Tharu Village, Thakurdwara

    Day 7→

    165 m

    1. A lighter day combining a village visit with a dedicated birding walk, useful for wide-angle habitat shots and smaller subjects between tiger searches.

    15 birds possible

    Birds: Baya Weaver, Black Drongo, Common Myna, Cattle Egret, Indian Pond-Heron, Red-wattled Lapwing, Scaly-breasted Munia, Indian Roller, Long-tailed Shrike, Spotted Owlet, Common Hoopoe, Green Bee-eater, Shikra, White-throated Kingfisher, Citrine Wagtail

    Butterflies: Plain Tiger

    Day 8→

    1,400 m · 1 night(s) · hotel

    1. A morning departure from Bardiya and the transfer to Nepalgunj for the flight back to Kathmandu.

    2. Stay: hotel. Hotel in Kathmandu

      An evening at leisure in Kathmandu to review images.

    19 birds possible

    Birds: Black Kite, Cattle Egret, Asian Pied Starling, Common Myna, Indian Pond-Heron, Black Drongo, Red-wattled Lapwing, Indian Roller, Spotted Owlet, Green Bee-eater, Long-tailed Shrike, Large-billed Crow, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

    Day 9→
