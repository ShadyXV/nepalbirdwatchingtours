Bardiya Wildlife Safari and Birdwatching

Four full days in Bardiya's tiger country, timed around the best light at dawn and dusk.

9 days and 79 birds recorded on this route.

Best months: February to May and October to November.

Start the journeyDay by day, on the map

1 / 5

Nine quiet days in Bardiya, Nepal's wildest lowland park, with far fewer people than Chitwan. Krishna plans each day around the dawn and dusk light: grassland and sal forest for Great Slaty Woodpeckers and hornbills, the Karnali for gharials, River Terns and Ibisbill, and patient waits where tigers cross. Made for photographers and wildlife lovers who like to wait for the moment.

A day on this trip

Jeep and walking safaris leave before sunrise and stay out until about midday, often with a long, quiet wait at a waterhole or a river bend. After lunch and a rest at the lodge we go out again for the evening light.

The way, day by day

  1. Day 1
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Large-billed Crow

    1. Airport pickup, hotel check-in, and a photography-focused briefing (gear checklist, safari etiquette, target species).

    Birds 10: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  2. Day 2
    Thakurdwara170 m · Cattle Egret, Asian Pied Starling

    1. A morning flight to Nepalgunj, then the drive to Bardiya National Park.

    2. Our base for six nights at the park's edge. An afternoon walk near the lodge to scout light conditions and river access points.

    Birds 21: Black Kite, Cattle Egret, Asian Pied Starling, Common Myna, Indian Pond-Heron, Black Drongo, Red-wattled Lapwing, Indian Roller, Spotted Owlet, Green Bee-eater, Long-tailed Shrike, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Coppersmith Barbet, Rufous Treepie, Rose-ringed Parakeet, Lineated Barbet, Greater Coucal, Indian Grey Hornbill, Common Hoopoe, Asian Koel

    Mammals 1: Rhesus Macaque

    Plants 1: Silk-cotton Tree

  3. Day 3
    Bardiya Grasslands180 m · Bengal Florican, Sarus Crane

    1. Four full days of jeep and walking safaris across different zones of the park, timed around dawn and dusk light for photography. We start in the open grasslands, where deer herds graze at first light and tigers hunt the edges.

    Birds 10: Indian Peafowl, Red Junglefowl, Plain Prinia, Black Francolin, Striated Grassbird, Chestnut-capped Babbler, Crested Serpent-Eagle, Bengal Florican, Sarus Crane, Pied Harrier

    Mammals 10: Spotted Deer, Wild Boar, Hog Deer, Swamp Deer, Nilgai, Northern Plains Gray Langur, Greater One-horned Rhinoceros, Asian Elephant, Bengal Tiger, Leopard

    Plants 1: Kans Grass

  4. Day 4
    Karnali River (Geruwa)155 m · River Tern, Ibisbill

    1. A river float on the Geruwa, the eastern channel of the Karnali, for gharial and riverine birds. From the banks we watch the open sand for tigers and elephants crossing in the evening light.

    Birds 17: River Lapwing, River Tern, Great Thick-knee, Pied Kingfisher, White-throated Kingfisher, Little Cormorant, Great Egret, Woolly-necked Stork, Oriental Darter, Crested Kingfisher, Asian Openbill, Grey-headed Fish-Eagle, Black-necked Stork, Brown Fish-Owl, Ruddy Shelduck, Common Sandpiper, Ibisbill

    Mammals 4: Ganges River Dolphin, Smooth-coated Otter, Asian Elephant, Bengal Tiger

    Reptiles 2: Gharial, Mugger Crocodile

  5. Day 5
    Sal Forest Interior200 m · Great Slaty Woodpecker, Oriental Pied Hornbill

    1. A full day by jeep deep into the sal forest, with long quiet waits at waterholes and clearings. Woodpeckers, hornbills and raptors keep the camera busy between tiger searches.

    Birds 16: Greater Racket-tailed Drongo, Jungle Babbler, Common Iora, Black-hooded Oriole, Great Slaty Woodpecker, Greater Flameback, Lesser Yellownape, Oriental Pied Hornbill, Small Minivet, Changeable Hawk-Eagle, Great Hornbill, Red-headed Vulture, White-rumped Vulture, White-rumped Shama, Indian Pitta, Asian Paradise-Flycatcher

    Mammals 8: Spotted Deer, Sambar, Barking Deer, Northern Plains Gray Langur, Asian Elephant, Bengal Tiger, Sloth Bear, Leopard

    Plants 1: Sal

  6. Day 6
    Riverine Forest, Karnali Floodplain160 m · Lesser Flameback, Fulvous-breasted Woodpecker

    1. A walking safari through the riverine forest and floodplain, quiet and close to the ground, with rhinos in the wet grass and kingfishers along the channels.

    Birds 10: Red Junglefowl, Jungle Babbler, White-throated Kingfisher, Lesser Flameback, Fulvous-breasted Woodpecker, Tickell's Blue Flycatcher, Lineated Barbet, Stork-billed Kingfisher, Brown Fish-Owl, Orange-headed Thrush

    Mammals 5: Rhesus Macaque, Spotted Deer, Greater One-horned Rhinoceros, Asian Elephant, Bengal Tiger

    Reptiles 2: Bengal Monitor, Indian Rock Python

    Butterflies 1: Common Mormon

  7. Day 7
    Tharu Village, Thakurdwara165 m · Baya Weaver, Scaly-breasted Munia

    1. A lighter day combining a village visit with a dedicated birding walk, useful for wide-angle habitat shots and smaller subjects between tiger searches.

    Birds 15: Baya Weaver, Black Drongo, Common Myna, Cattle Egret, Indian Pond-Heron, Red-wattled Lapwing, Scaly-breasted Munia, Indian Roller, Long-tailed Shrike, Spotted Owlet, Common Hoopoe, Green Bee-eater, Shikra, White-throated Kingfisher, Citrine Wagtail

    Butterflies 1: Plain Tiger

  8. Day 8
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Cattle Egret

    1. A morning departure from Bardiya and the transfer to Nepalgunj for the flight back to Kathmandu.

    2. An evening at leisure in Kathmandu to review images.

    Birds 19: Black Kite, Cattle Egret, Asian Pied Starling, Common Myna, Indian Pond-Heron, Black Drongo, Red-wattled Lapwing, Indian Roller, Spotted Owlet, Green Bee-eater, Long-tailed Shrike, Large-billed Crow, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  9. Day 9
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Large-billed Crow

    1. Final breakfast and the departure transfer.

    Birds 10: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)
