Mardi Himal Birdwatching Trek

Up a rhododendron ridge to the foot of Machhapuchhre, with time for every flock in the forest.

9 days, up to 4,500 m, and 87 birds recorded on this route.

Best months: March to May and October to November.

Start the journeyDay by day, on the map

1 / 5

A short, quiet trek up a rhododendron ridge to the foot of Machhapuchhre, with more forest per day than any other trek near Pokhara. Krishna gives the forest camps time for sibias, laughingthrushes, Mrs. Gould's Sunbird and calling pheasants, then climbs to 4,500 m for Snow Pigeons, Lammergeier and the sunrise. A good first Himalayan birding trek.

A day on this trip

I wake you at first light for the forest flocks, then we walk three to five hours up the ridge, slowly, stopping for every flock. The camps are small and quiet, and the evenings are for the light on Machhapuchhre.

The way, day by day

  1. Day 1
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Large-billed Crow

    1. Airport pickup, hotel check-in and the trip briefing.

    Birds 10: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  2. Day 2
    Pokhara, Phewa Lake800 m · Little Cormorant, Indian Pond-Heron

    1. Travel to Pokhara by road or a short flight, and an evening by Phewa Lake.

    Birds 12: Little Cormorant, Indian Pond-Heron, Cattle Egret, White-throated Kingfisher, Black Kite, Red-vented Bulbul, Pied Kingfisher, Common Kingfisher, Little Egret, Himalayan Griffon, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Great Cormorant

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m)

  3. Day 3
    Forest Camp2,520 m · Spiny Babbler, Himalayan Cutia

    1. Drive to Kande and begin the trek, climbing straight into birding-rich forest.

    2. The first overnight stop, deep in the forest. The last hour of light and the first hour of morning here are for mixed flocks of sibias, yuhinas and minlas, and Kalij Pheasants on the trail.

    Birds 31: Himalayan Bulbul, Great Barbet, Grey-hooded Warbler, Oriental White-eye, Black-throated Tit, Grey Treepie, Slaty-headed Parakeet, Verditer Flycatcher, Long-tailed Minivet, Black Bulbul, Himalayan Griffon, Spiny Babbler, Rufous Sibia, Whiskered Yuhina, Green-backed Tit, Blue-winged Minla, Chestnut-crowned Laughingthrush, Kalij Pheasant, Striated Laughingthrush, White-throated Laughingthrush, Rufous-winged Fulvetta, Nepal Fulvetta, Mrs. Gould's Sunbird, Scarlet Minivet, Yellow-billed Blue Magpie, Koklass Pheasant, Hill Partridge, Himalayan Cutia, Great Parrotbill, Himalayan Cuckoo, Large Hawk-Cuckoo

    Mammals 2: Himalayan Langur, Barking Deer

    Plants 1: Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  4. Day 4
    Low Camp2,970 m · Satyr Tragopan, Fire-tailed Sunbird

    1. A full birding-and-trekking day through dense rhododendron and oak forest, prime habitat for pheasants and laughingthrushes.

    Birds 18: Rufous-vented Tit, Grey-crested Tit, Stripe-throated Yuhina, Chestnut-tailed Minla, White-browed Fulvetta, Spotted Laughingthrush, Hoary-throated Barwing, Spotted Nutcracker, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Yellow-browed Tit, Bar-tailed Treecreeper, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Collared Grosbeak, Himalayan Monal, Koklass Pheasant, Great Parrotbill, Satyr Tragopan, Fire-tailed Sunbird

    Mammals 1: Yellow-throated Marten

    Plants 2: Bearded Rhododendron, Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  5. Day 5
    High Camp3,580 m · Blood Pheasant, Lammergeier

    1. Continue ascending as forest gives way to rhododendron scrub and open ridgeline, watching for high-altitude species and raptors.

    Birds 14: Rufous-breasted Accentor, White-browed Fulvetta, Large-billed Crow, Yellow-billed Chough, Himalayan Monal, Blood Pheasant, White-collared Blackbird, Dark-breasted Rosefinch, Himalayan White-browed Rosefinch, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Golden Eagle, Alpine Accentor, Grandala

    Mammals 1: Himalayan Tahr

    Plants 2: Bell-flowered Rhododendron, Lepidote Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  6. Day 6
    Mardi Himal Base Camp · Low Camp2,970 m · Snow Pigeon, Red-billed Chough

    1. An early start for the viewpoint at Mardi Himal Base Camp (4,500 m) and sunrise views of Machhapuchhre and Annapurna South. Snow Pigeons, choughs and accentors work the slopes around us.

    2. Then we descend the ridge to Low Camp for the night, with a second chance at the forest birds.

    Birds 30: Snow Pigeon, Yellow-billed Chough, Red-billed Chough, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Plain Mountain Finch, Himalayan Snowcock, Golden Eagle, White-winged Redstart, Wallcreeper, Alpine Accentor, Grandala, Rufous-vented Tit, Grey-crested Tit, Stripe-throated Yuhina, Chestnut-tailed Minla, White-browed Fulvetta, Spotted Laughingthrush, Hoary-throated Barwing, Spotted Nutcracker, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Yellow-browed Tit, Bar-tailed Treecreeper, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Collared Grosbeak, Himalayan Monal, Koklass Pheasant, Great Parrotbill, Satyr Tragopan, Fire-tailed Sunbird

    Mammals 3: Himalayan Tahr, Large-eared Pika, Yellow-throated Marten

    Butterflies 1: Indian Tortoiseshell

    Plants 2: Bearded Rhododendron, Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  7. Day 7
    Siding1,750 m · Plumbeous Water Redstart, Blue Whistling-Thrush

    1. Trek down through the forest again, this time in daylight for full birding coverage, ending at Siding village.

    Birds 16: Grey Treepie, Himalayan Bulbul, Black Bulbul, Plumbeous Water Redstart, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Great Barbet, Blue Whistling-Thrush, Slaty-headed Parakeet, Grey Wagtail, Spotted Forktail, Kalij Pheasant, Brown Dipper, Verditer Flycatcher, Spiny Babbler, Crested Kingfisher, White-capped Redstart

    Mammals 1: Himalayan Goral

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  8. Day 8
    Pokhara800 m · Red-wattled Lapwing, Little Cormorant

    1. A short walk out to the roadhead and the drive back to Pokhara, with a free afternoon by the lake.

    Birds 9: Little Cormorant, White-throated Kingfisher, Black Kite, Red-vented Bulbul, Cattle Egret, Pied Kingfisher, Himalayan Griffon, Red-wattled Lapwing, Oriental Magpie-Robin

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m)

  9. Day 9
    Kathmandu1,400 m · Black Kite, Large-billed Crow

    1. Return to Kathmandu by road or air, and the departure transfer.

    Birds 10: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)
