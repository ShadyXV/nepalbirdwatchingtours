Mardi Himal Birdwatching Trek

Up a rhododendron ridge to the foot of Machhapuchhre, with time for every flock in the forest.

The Mardi Himal Trek is one of Nepal's most scenic short treks, and its dense rhododendron and oak forest sections are exceptional for birding. This 9-day itinerary pairs the classic trek to Mardi Himal Base Camp with dedicated birding time in the forest zones.

Highlights

  • Close-up views of Machhapuchhre (Fish Tail) and the Annapurna range
  • Rich rhododendron forest birding: laughingthrushes, minivets, and Himalayan pheasants
  • Base Camp viewpoint at 4,500 m
  • Moderate difficulty, suitable for trekkers with some hiking experience

The way, day by day

  1. Day 1Kathmandu

    Day 1→

    1,400 m · 1 night(s) · hotel

    1. Stay: hotel. Hotel in Kathmandu

      Airport pickup, hotel check-in and the trip briefing.

    10 birds possible

    Birds: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  2. Day 2Pokhara, Phewa Lake

    Day 2→

    800 m · 1 night(s) · hotel

    1. Stay: hotel. Lakeside hotel by Phewa Lake

      Travel to Pokhara by road or a short flight, and an evening by Phewa Lake.

    12 birds possible

    Birds: Little Cormorant, Indian Pond-Heron, Cattle Egret, White-throated Kingfisher, Black Kite, Red-vented Bulbul, Pied Kingfisher, Common Kingfisher, Little Egret, Himalayan Griffon, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Great Cormorant

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m)

  3. Day 3Forest Camp

    Day 3→

    2,520 m · 1 night(s) · teahouse

    1. Drive to Kande and begin the trek, climbing straight into birding-rich forest.

    2. Stay: teahouse. Small teahouse in a forest clearing

      The first overnight stop, deep in the forest. The last hour of light and the first hour of morning here are for mixed flocks of sibias, yuhinas and minlas, and Kalij Pheasants on the trail.

    31 birds possible

    Birds: Himalayan Bulbul, Great Barbet, Grey-hooded Warbler, Oriental White-eye, Black-throated Tit, Grey Treepie, Slaty-headed Parakeet, Verditer Flycatcher, Long-tailed Minivet, Black Bulbul, Himalayan Griffon, Spiny Babbler, Rufous Sibia, Whiskered Yuhina, Green-backed Tit, Blue-winged Minla, Chestnut-crowned Laughingthrush, Kalij Pheasant, Striated Laughingthrush, White-throated Laughingthrush, Rufous-winged Fulvetta, Nepal Fulvetta, Mrs. Gould's Sunbird, Scarlet Minivet, Yellow-billed Blue Magpie, Koklass Pheasant, Hill Partridge, Himalayan Cutia, Great Parrotbill, Himalayan Cuckoo, Large Hawk-Cuckoo

    Mammals: Himalayan Langur, Barking Deer

    Plants: Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  4. Day 4Low Camp

    Day 4→

    2,970 m · 1 night(s) · teahouse

    1. Stay: teahouse. Teahouse at the top of the forest, with a first view of Machhapuchhre

      A full birding-and-trekking day through dense rhododendron and oak forest, prime habitat for pheasants and laughingthrushes.

    18 birds possible

    Birds: Rufous-vented Tit, Grey-crested Tit, Stripe-throated Yuhina, Chestnut-tailed Minla, White-browed Fulvetta, Spotted Laughingthrush, Hoary-throated Barwing, Spotted Nutcracker, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Yellow-browed Tit, Bar-tailed Treecreeper, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Collared Grosbeak, Himalayan Monal, Koklass Pheasant, Great Parrotbill, Satyr Tragopan, Fire-tailed Sunbird

    Mammals: Yellow-throated Marten

    Plants: Bearded Rhododendron, Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  5. Day 5High Camp

    Day 5→

    3,580 m · 1 night(s) · teahouse

    1. Stay: teahouse. Basic teahouse on the open ridge; cold nights

      Continue ascending as forest gives way to rhododendron scrub and open ridgeline, watching for high-altitude species and raptors.

    14 birds possible

    Birds: Rufous-breasted Accentor, White-browed Fulvetta, Large-billed Crow, Yellow-billed Chough, Himalayan Monal, Blood Pheasant, White-collared Blackbird, Dark-breasted Rosefinch, Himalayan White-browed Rosefinch, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Golden Eagle, Alpine Accentor, Grandala

    Mammals: Himalayan Tahr

    Plants: Bell-flowered Rhododendron, Lepidote Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  6. Day 6Low Camp

    Day 6→

    2,970 m · 1 night(s) · teahouse

    1. An early start for the viewpoint at Mardi Himal Base Camp (4,500 m) and sunrise views of Machhapuchhre and Annapurna South. Snow Pigeons, choughs and accentors work the slopes around us.

    2. Stay: teahouse. Back at the Low Camp teahouse

      Then we descend the ridge to Low Camp for the night, with a second chance at the forest birds.

    30 birds possible

    Birds: Snow Pigeon, Yellow-billed Chough, Red-billed Chough, Lammergeier, Himalayan Griffon, Plain Mountain Finch, Himalayan Snowcock, Golden Eagle, White-winged Redstart, Wallcreeper, Alpine Accentor, Grandala, Rufous-vented Tit, Grey-crested Tit, Stripe-throated Yuhina, Chestnut-tailed Minla, White-browed Fulvetta, Spotted Laughingthrush, Hoary-throated Barwing, Spotted Nutcracker, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Yellow-browed Tit, Bar-tailed Treecreeper, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Collared Grosbeak, Himalayan Monal, Koklass Pheasant, Great Parrotbill, Satyr Tragopan, Fire-tailed Sunbird

    Mammals: Himalayan Tahr, Large-eared Pika, Yellow-throated Marten

    Butterflies: Indian Tortoiseshell

    Plants: Bearded Rhododendron, Tree Rhododendron

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  7. Day 7Siding

    Day 7→

    1,750 m · 1 night(s) · homestay

    1. Stay: homestay. Village homestay or small lodge

      Trek down through the forest again, this time in daylight for full birding coverage, ending at Siding village.

    16 birds possible

    Birds: Grey Treepie, Himalayan Bulbul, Black Bulbul, Plumbeous Water Redstart, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Great Barbet, Blue Whistling-Thrush, Slaty-headed Parakeet, Grey Wagtail, Spotted Forktail, Kalij Pheasant, Brown Dipper, Verditer Flycatcher, Spiny Babbler, Crested Kingfisher, White-capped Redstart

    Mammals: Himalayan Goral

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m)

  8. Day 8Pokhara

    Day 8→

    800 m · 1 night(s) · hotel

    1. Stay: hotel. Lakeside hotel by Phewa Lake

      A short walk out to the roadhead and the drive back to Pokhara, with a free afternoon by the lake.

    9 birds possible

    Birds: Little Cormorant, White-throated Kingfisher, Black Kite, Red-vented Bulbul, Cattle Egret, Pied Kingfisher, Himalayan Griffon, Red-wattled Lapwing, Oriental Magpie-Robin

    Seen from here: Machhapuchhre (6,993 m), Annapurna South (7,219 m), Annapurna I (8,091 m), Hiunchuli (6,441 m)

  9. Day 9Kathmandu

    Day 9→

    1,400 m

    1. Return to Kathmandu by road or air, and the departure transfer.

    10 birds possible

    Birds: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)
