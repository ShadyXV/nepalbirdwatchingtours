Red Panda Trek

Ten days in the bamboo cloud forest of eastern Nepal, searching for the red panda at dawn and dusk.

Start the journey10 days · Day by day, on the map

Ilam, eastern Nepal

Best months
Mar, Apr, May, Oct, Nov, Dec

Eastern Nepal's Ilam region holds some of the country's best remaining red panda habitat, alongside outstanding birding in remote rhododendron and bamboo forest. This 10-day trek is designed for wildlife photographers seeking one of the Himalayas' most elusive mammals.

Highlights

  • Search for wild red pandas in prime bamboo-rhododendron habitat
  • Remote eastern Nepal trekking route, far from crowded trails
  • Rich birdlife including Himalayan pheasants and high-altitude specialists
  • Guided by naturalists experienced in red panda tracking

The way, day by day

  1. Day 1Kathmandu

    Day 1→

    1,400 m · 1 night(s) · hotel

    1. Stay: hotel. Hotel in Kathmandu

      Airport pickup, hotel check-in, and a photography and trekking briefing.

    10 birds possible

    Birds: Black Kite, Large-billed Crow, Common Myna, Oriental Magpie-Robin, Red-vented Bulbul, Spotted Dove, Himalayan Bulbul, Eurasian Kestrel, Grey Wagtail, Egyptian Vulture

    Seen from here: Phulchowki (2,782 m)

  2. Day 2Ilam

    Day 2→

    1,200 m · 1 night(s) · homestay

    1. A morning flight to Bhadrapur, in the far eastern lowlands of Nepal.

    2. Stay: homestay. Homestay or small hotel among the tea gardens

      Then the drive into the tea-growing hills of Ilam. The tea gardens and forest patches around town are a good first look at the birds of the eastern hills.

    25 birds possible

    Birds: Black Kite, Cattle Egret, Asian Pied Starling, Common Myna, Indian Pond-Heron, Black Drongo, Red-wattled Lapwing, Indian Roller, Spotted Owlet, White-throated Kingfisher, Green Bee-eater, Himalayan Bulbul, Red-vented Bulbul, Great Barbet, Grey Treepie, Oriental White-eye, Long-tailed Shrike, Golden-throated Barbet, Blue-throated Barbet, Verditer Flycatcher, Black Bulbul, Common Tailorbird, Long-tailed Minivet, Grey-hooded Warbler, Mountain Hawk-Eagle

    Mammals: Assamese Macaque

  3. Day 3Dobate

    Day 3→

    2,660 m · 1 night(s) · homestay

    1. Stay: homestay. Community homestay in the village

      We drive to the trailhead and begin trekking into forested hill terrain, up to the small village of Dobate on the edge of the red panda forest.

    15 birds possible

    Birds: Rufous Sibia, Whiskered Yuhina, Blue-winged Minla, Green-backed Tit, Chestnut-crowned Laughingthrush, Kalij Pheasant, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Rufous-bellied Niltava, Large Niltava, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Yellow-billed Blue Magpie, Black-throated Parrotbill, Hill Partridge, Satyr Tragopan, Scarlet Finch

    Mammals: Barking Deer, Red Panda, Yellow-throated Marten

    Plants: Maling Bamboo, Tree Rhododendron

  4. Day 4Bamboo Forest Camp

    Day 4→

    2,950 m · 1 night(s) · camp

    1. Stay: camp. Tented camp or a simple shelter, as conditions allow

      The first of two days of trekking through dense bamboo-rhododendron forest, the preferred habitat of red pandas.

    15 birds possible

    Birds: White-browed Fulvetta, Rufous-vented Tit, Stripe-throated Yuhina, Chestnut-tailed Minla, Black-faced Laughingthrush, Black-throated Parrotbill, Hoary-throated Barwing, Spotted Laughingthrush, Rufous-vented Yuhina, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Red-headed Bullfinch, Satyr Tragopan, Great Parrotbill, Fire-tailed Myzornis, Gold-naped Finch

    Mammals: Himalayan Langur, Red Panda, Himalayan Black Bear

    Plants: Maling Bamboo, Bearded Rhododendron

  5. Day 5Red Panda Habitat, Sandakpur Ridge

    Day 5→

    3,100 m · 4 night(s) · camp

    1. Stay: camp. Base for four nights in the core habitat; tented camp or simple shelter

      We reach the core habitat, with naturalist guides tracking signs (droppings, feeding marks) to locate active areas. This is our base for the search days.

    18 birds possible

    Birds: White-browed Fulvetta, Grey-crested Tit, Rufous-vented Tit, Black-faced Laughingthrush, Large-billed Crow, Golden-breasted Fulvetta, Black-throated Parrotbill, Red-headed Bullfinch, Dark-breasted Rosefinch, Collared Grosbeak, Spotted Laughingthrush, Darjeeling Woodpecker, Fire-tailed Sunbird, Satyr Tragopan, Blood Pheasant, Himalayan Monal, Fire-tailed Myzornis, Scarlet Finch

    Mammals: Yellow-throated Marten, Himalayan Serow, Red Panda, Himalayan Black Bear

    Plants: Maling Bamboo, Bell-flowered Rhododendron

    Day 6→

    Mammals: Himalayan Serow, Red Panda
