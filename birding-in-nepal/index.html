Birding in Nepal

Nepal has around 880 species of birds, about 8% of all the birds in the world. The land rises from about 60 m in the Terai lowlands to 8,849 m at the top of Everest, so one trip can take you from hornbills in the sal forest to monals on an alpine ridge. The main birding season runs from October to May.

I'm Krishna Thapa, a birding guide from Kathmandu. Here is where to go, the birds to expect and the best months for each place, with the trips I lead.

Why is Nepal so good for birds?

Nepal sits where the birds of the Indian lowlands meet those of the Himalaya and the Tibetan plateau. One trip can climb from forest full of hornbills and kingfishers, through misty rhododendron loud with laughingthrushes, to alpine ridges where lammergeiers sail.

One bird lives nowhere else: the Spiny Babbler, Nepal's only endemic, singing in the scrub around Kathmandu.

Birding fits easily around a safari, a trek or Kathmandu's temples.

Where to go birding in Nepal

From the lowland parks in the south to the high valleys under the Himalaya: the places I go back to most, with their birds, best months and trips.

Phulchowki and the Kathmandu Valley

Best months: February to April and October to November

A short drive from the city, Phulchowki climbs through oak and rhododendron forest to 2,782 m: the best birding near Kathmandu, for laughingthrushes, niltavas, sunbirds and warblers. Half a day is enough; Shivapuri, across the valley, makes another fine morning.

Chitwan National Park

Best months: October to March

Nepal's first national park, a World Heritage Site, and one of Asia's best lowland birding spots. Sal forest, grassland and rivers hold hornbills, kingfishers, storks and raptors, Bengal Florican displaying in spring, and rhinos, gharials and, with luck, a tiger.

Pokhara and the Annapurna foothills

Best months: March to May and October to November

Pokhara's lakes hold wintering ducks and kingfishers. Above them rises the Annapurna Conservation Area, Nepal's largest: on the trails to the Sanctuary and Mardi Himal look for Himalayan Monal, the national bird, Satyr Tragopan and lammergeiers, under Machhapuchhre.

Langtang and Gosaikunda

Best months: March to May and October to November

The high mountains closest to Kathmandu. Trails climb from warm gorges through rhododendron and fir to Gosaikunda's sacred lakes at 4,380 m. Red pandas live in the forest, Ibisbill on the river shingle, Wallcreepers on the rock faces.

Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve

Best months: November to February

Nepal's best-known wetland, on the braided Sapta Koshi river in the east. In winter its marshes and sandbars fill with ducks, waders, terns and raptors, wild water buffalo graze the edges and the wet grass hides the Swamp Francolin.

Ilam and the far east

Best months: March to May and October to December

Red panda forest on the Sandakpur ridge above Ilam's tea gardens, with Kanchenjunga, the world's third highest mountain, on the skyline. The bamboo and rhododendron hold parrotbills, fulvettas, Fire-tailed Myzornis and Satyr Tragopan: slow, patient birding.

Bardia, Lumbini and the western lowlands

Best months: February to May and October to November

Chitwan's wilder, quieter cousin: sal forest and grassland on the Karnali river. Great Slaty Woodpecker, hornbills and River Terns share it with tigers, elephants and river dolphins. Near Lumbini, the Buddha's birthplace, the wetlands hold Sarus Cranes and waterbirds.

When is the best time to go birding?

The main birding season runs from October to May: spring and autumn in the mountains, winter in the lowlands, when the wetlands are full. Tell me the birds you most want to see and I'll suggest the dates.

AutumnOctober to November

Clear skies after the monsoon and the best mountain views. Migrants pass through the valleys; it's the main trekking season, so trails are busy.

WinterDecember to February

Lowland wetlands fill with ducks, waders and raptors; mountain birds come down to the hills around Kathmandu. Cold nights, snow on the high passes.

SpringMarch to May

Breeding season: birds in song and colour, pheasants calling, rhododendron in flower, Bengal Florican displaying in the Terai. The lowlands are hot by May.

MonsoonJune to September

Rain most days, cloud on the peaks, leeches in the forest, and many birds nesting. With flexible plans it's good birding; I guide all year.

Photographers, artists and trekkers

Photographers

The best light and the most birds come in the first two hours after sunrise and the last hour before dark, so the days are built around them. In Chitwan and Bardia much of it is waiting: at a waterhole, in a canoe drifting at dawn. In the hills we walk slowly and let the flocks come through.

Field support

Artists

I paint birds myself, so there is never a rush to the next spot. If a bird sits well we stay with it, long enough to watch how it moves and sketch. Most afternoons are quiet, good for the morning's drawings.

Trekkers
